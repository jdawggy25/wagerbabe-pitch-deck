## Scaling Structure

As your book grows, rates adjust predictably based on your tier:

### Tier 1 (1,000 player base)
| Player Count | Rate |
|--------------|------|
| 0 - 1,000 | $2.00/head |
| 1,001 - 2,000 | $3.00/head |
| 2,001+ | $4.00/head (capped) |

### Tiers 2-4 (500 player threshold)
| Player Count | Rate Adjustment |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Base capacity | Tier rate |
| +500 players | +$1.00/head |
| +1,000 players | +$1.00/head (capped) |

**Example:** Tier 1 investor with 2,500 players pays $2.00 for first 1,000, $3.00 for next 1,000, and $4.00 for the remaining 500.

**Example:** Tier 2 investor with 1,200 players pays $3.50 for first 500, $4.50 for next 500, and $5.50 for the remaining 200.
